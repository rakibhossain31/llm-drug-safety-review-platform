# Synthetic PV Guidance: Follow-up

Follow-up should be specific, proportionate, and focused on information needed to evaluate the case.

## Priority information
Request missing patient demographics, reporter contact details, suspect product dose, route, indication, therapy dates, event onset, outcome, seriousness details, medical history, concomitant medicines, relevant tests, treatment, dechallenge, and rechallenge information.

## Question quality
Questions should be plain, non-leading, and traceable to a missing data field. Avoid requesting direct identifiers that are not necessary for pharmacovigilance follow-up. Human reviewers should prioritize questions according to case risk and local procedures.
