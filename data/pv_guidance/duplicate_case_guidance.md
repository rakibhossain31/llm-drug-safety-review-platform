# Synthetic PV Guidance: Duplicate Case Review

Duplicate detection is a review-support activity. Similarity alone must not automatically merge or delete cases.

## Comparison fields
Compare patient characteristics, reporter, suspect and concomitant products, adverse events, therapy dates, event onset, country, literature citation, and narrative details. A high text similarity score is a triage signal, not a final duplicate decision.

## Review action
Present likely matches with the contributing similarities. A human reviewer should compare source documents, preserve the audit trail, and document whether records are duplicates, follow-ups, or distinct cases.
