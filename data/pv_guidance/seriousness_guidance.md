# Synthetic PV Guidance: Seriousness Assessment

This document supports education only and is not regulatory advice.

## Seriousness criteria
A case may be serious when the event results in death, is life-threatening, causes or prolongs hospitalization, causes persistent or significant disability, involves a congenital anomaly, or is another medically important condition. Seriousness is different from clinical severity.

## Hospitalization
Confirm whether admission was caused by the adverse event, whether an existing admission was prolonged, and record admission and discharge dates. Emergency room attendance without admission may require medical judgment rather than automatic classification.

## Human review
Automated keyword detection is only a signal. A qualified human reviewer must make and document the final assessment.
